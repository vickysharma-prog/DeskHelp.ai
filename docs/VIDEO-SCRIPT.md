# Video script

Three minutes. Read this off the second screen; record the first.

**The call:** Fee reminder (before due date) → **S. Verma**
**The handset:** the second phone, on speaker

---

## Before you press record

- [ ] Browser fullscreen (F11), only the DeskHelp tab
- [ ] Phone on speaker, beside the laptop, not on it
- [ ] Do Not Disturb on
- [ ] Signed in, sitting on the **Dashboard**
- [ ] OBS: Desktop Audio muted, Noise Suppression on the mic

---

## Part 1 — The product (0:00 to 1:30)

### 0:00 Dashboard

> "This is DeskHelp. Every week an institute's front office works through the
> same list of phone calls. Fees due. A child missing from the morning batch.
> Twenty people who asked about admission in June and were never called back.
> DeskHelp makes those calls and tells you what each one settled."

Point at the numbers on screen: 124 families, four workflows on, questions
waiting.

### 0:25 Workflows

Click **Workflows**. Scroll slowly.

> "Fourteen kinds of call. Fees, attendance, admissions, parents, staff. Every
> one of them ships switched off, because installing this should never be the
> same thing as starting to ring people. The institute decides what it runs."

### 0:45 Dry run

Click **Dry run** on Fee reminder. Let the panel open. Scroll to the words.

> "Before anybody hears it, you can read it. This is the exact instruction the
> caller follows."

Scroll to the boundary section.

> "And this is what it is forbidden to do. No discount, no advice, no payment
> details, ever. It records nothing and dials nobody."

Close.

### 1:10 Review queue

Click **Review queue**.

> "When somebody asks a question the caller has no approved answer for, it does
> not guess. It says a person will confirm, and it writes the question down
> word for word. You answer it once, here, and the next call to that family
> delivers your answer."

---

## Part 2 — A real call (1:30 to 2:40)

### 1:30 Place it

Click **Workflows** → **Call now** on Fee reminder → choose **S. Verma**.

> "So let us actually call somebody."

Show the button naming her and the number. Press it.

> "One press, one call, one person. And the number has to be on the allow list
> before anything can ring at all."

**Pick up when it rings. Let it finish speaking before you answer, every time.**

### 1:45 On the call

| It says | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "…automated reminder… no payment is taken on this call." | *(say nothing, let it finish)* |
| "Kya main S. Verma se baat kar raha hoon?" | **"Haan ji, main S. Verma bol raha hoon."** |
| asks about the due date | **"Haan, pata hai. Fees kab tak deni hai?"** |
| quotes the 15th | **"Achha. Scholarship milti hai kya acche marks pe?"** |
| refuses, offers a callback | **"Aap bata do na, aapko toh pata hoga."** |
| refuses again | **"Theek hai. Main Friday tak pay kar dunga."** |
| asks how you will pay | **"Online portal se."** |

Then let it close.

**The two things that matter:** confirm your name plainly, and never talk over
it. Speaking across its opening makes it start again.

### 2:15 Read the call back

Click **Calls**. Your call is at the top. Click **Read the call**.

> "Here is the whole conversation. What the office said, what the parent said."

Scroll to the answers.

> "These are the answers it was willing to keep. Every one of them checked
> against what was actually said. If a quote does not appear in the parent's
> own words, it is thrown away and the call goes to a person."

Scroll to the claim.

> "And this is the important one. 'I will pay by Friday' is stored as something
> they said, with their words attached. It never marks the fee as paid. A
> promise is not a payment."

### 2:30 The queue

Click **Review queue**. Point at the scholarship question.

> "And the question it would not answer is already here, in the parent's own
> words, waiting for somebody to answer once."

## Part 3 — Close (2:40 to 3:00)

Back to the Dashboard, or the landing page.

> "It is built for schools, colleges and coaching institutes, and it works in
> any office that runs on the phone. Calls go out through CALL-E. Everything
> about who to call, when, what may be said, and what the answer meant is
> DeskHelp's."
>
> "Now your agent can call on behalf of you."

## If the call does not connect

It has happened four times out of six. Stop recording, say so, and try again.
Do not stitch a failed call into the take. There are fourteen calls left.

If it fails twice in a row, record Part 1 and Part 3 clean, and use the call
already sitting in **Calls** from 13 Sept, 12:07 for Part 2, narrating it as a
call that was placed from this screen. It was.
